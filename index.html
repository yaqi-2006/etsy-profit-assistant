Accounting methodology & scope limits ›

The English/USD 8-column monthly-statement profile is tested with synthetic files against a public historical format, not real shop exports. Multi-shop, mixed-currency, SKU cost models, and automatic Etsy sync are not supported. Without verified coverage, do not treat results as reconciled or final net profit. Order contribution isolates direct order economics; it intentionally excludes shared period advertising, overhead, and income tax.

03 / PRIVACY & LIMITS

Data stays in this session

No server-side analysis, telemetry, or default storage. Data is held in memory and cleared on refresh or close. Reports export only when you choose; clearing also removes this session's cost inputs.

Exports are not backups; analysis sessions cannot be restored. Do not enter real sensitive data in this unvalidated demo.

Statement interpretation rules & boundaries ›

Sales row net is not seller revenue before fees, and this is not order contribution, available payout or business profit. No order matches are inferred. Statement imports replace this session; duplicate-looking rows within a file are kept.
